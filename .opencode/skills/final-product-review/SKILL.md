# Final Product Review
Review hierarchy, spacing, typography, alignment, consistency, color, density, unnecessary cards/effects and generic AI patterns.
Review navigation, discoverability, feedback, loading, errors, empty states and interactions.
Review TypeScript, imports, runtime errors, duplication, dependencies, architecture and Electron boundaries.
Ask: does this look like a real product or an AI-generated template? If generic, refine it.
