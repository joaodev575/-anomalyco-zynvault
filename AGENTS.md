# Zyntrix OpenCode Design Rules

Treat Zyntrix as a real commercial desktop product.

Before implementing UI:
1. Inspect the existing application.
2. Understand the user task.
3. Inspect existing components and styles.
4. Identify generic/AI-looking patterns.
5. Establish hierarchy and composition.
6. Reuse the design system.
7. Implement carefully.
8. Review visually and technically.
9. Refine anything generic.
10. Preserve functionality unless explicitly asked otherwise.

Do not blindly generate dashboard card grids, gradients, glassmorphism, huge
headings, excessive rounded corners, unnecessary icons/badges, decorative
backgrounds, excessive shadows or generic SaaS layouts.

Zyntrix direction: dark professional desktop aesthetic, controlled blue
identity, strong typography, useful information density, subtle borders,
restrained effects and precise alignment.

Modern does not mean more effects. Modern means better hierarchy,
composition, interaction, consistency and product thinking.
